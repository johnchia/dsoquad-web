Vertical calibration

Stored on the DSO itself, so it follows the device to any computer. Nothing is written until you press Save to device.

1 · Zero

Short CH A and CH B to 0 V: clip each probe tip to its own ground lead (open inputs read ~20 mV off). Then measure: it steps through all 8 ranges at 3 positions on both channels (about 10 s).

2 · Gain (optional, needs a known DC voltage)

Connect a DC voltage you know to one channel. The wave out works: connect it to the channel, press Wave out high and measure it with a multimeter. Each run calibrates the ranges where the voltage shows as 1.5–7.6 divisions, so use several voltages to cover every range.

Firmware update

Installs over USB in a few seconds: no DFU mode, no buttons. The DSO restarts into the new firmware and this page reconnects. Calibration is kept.
